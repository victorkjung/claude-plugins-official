# Investment Memo: 24-Unit Multifamily, Albany NY

_Fictional sample for testing doc-eval._

## Recommendation
Approve a **$3.1M** acquisition of the Maple Court apartments, funded with **$1.0M equity** and a
**$2.1M** agency loan (6.4% fixed, 30-year amortization). Close by **December 15, 2026**.

## Thesis
Maple Court is under-managed: in-place rents average **$1,180**, about **14% below** comparable
renovated units within a mile ($1,370, per the attached rent survey of 9 properties). Renovating
units on turnover and professionalizing management should lift NOI from **$198K** to **$262K**
within 30 months.

## The asset
- 24 units (16 one-bed, 8 two-bed), built 1978, 93% occupied
- Roofs replaced 2021; boilers are original and budgeted for replacement in year 2

## Financials
| | In place | Year 3 (stabilized) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gross rent | $340K | $395K |
| Operating expenses | $142K | $133K |
| NOI | $198K | $262K |
| Cap rate on price | 6.4% | 8.5% |

Renovation budget: **$18K per unit** on turnover (about 8 units per year), plus **$90K** for boilers.

## Risks
- **Renovation pace:** turnover could run slower than 8 units per year. At 5 per year, year-3 NOI is $238K.
- **Rates:** refinancing in year 5 at 7.5% instead of 6.4% reduces the levered IRR from 16% to 12%.
- **Regulation:** New York rent law changes could cap increases on renewals; the plan assumes gains
  come mostly from renovated turnover units rather than renewals.

## Returns
Base case: **16% levered IRR**, **1.9x equity multiple** over 5 years. Downside (slow renovation,
higher rates): **9% IRR**, **1.4x**.

## Ask and next steps
Approve **$1.0M equity** today. Next steps: sign the purchase agreement by October 20, then complete
the property inspection and the lender appraisal by November 15.
