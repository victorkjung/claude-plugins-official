# Residential Lease Agreement

_Fictional sample for testing doc-eval._

This Lease is made between **Harbor Lane Properties LLC** ("Landlord") and **Jordan Ellis** ("Tenant")
for the premises at **118 Harbor Lane, Unit 4B, Brooklyn, NY 11201** ("Premises").

## 1. Term
The lease begins on **October 1, 2026** and ends on **September 30, 2027**.

## 2. Rent
Tenant will pay **$2,850 per month**, due on the **1st of each month**, through the tenant portal.
A late fee applies if rent is not received on time.

## 3. Security Deposit
Tenant will pay a security deposit of **$2,850** before move-in. The deposit will be returned after
move-out, less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## 4. Utilities
Landlord pays water and trash. Tenant pays electricity, gas, and internet.

## 5. Maintenance and Repairs
Tenant must keep the Premises clean and report maintenance issues promptly. Landlord will make
necessary repairs within a reasonable time. Tenant is responsible for damage caused by Tenant or guests.

## 6. Entry
Landlord may enter the Premises with reasonable notice to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit,
and at any time in an emergency.

## 7. Pets
No pets without Landlord's written consent.

## 8. Renewal and Termination
Landlord will offer a renewal or give notice of non-renewal as required by law. Tenant must give
written notice if Tenant does not intend to renew.
